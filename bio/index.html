in my own words · from linkedin

Technological Product Leader, Expert Developer, App Entrepreneur, and Manager of IT Projects by drive and profession.

I am currently the Principal Product Engineer for Web & Android at Endavu, actively working on the best investment platform in Denmark (soon to be beyond). I have contributed to or led development on more than 20 app projects and am a self-publisher with over 1,000,000 downloads on personal projects.

My story is long, but the short version is this: I studied computer science, started making apps as an Android developer, and then began leading others and broadening out. I love developing digital services and have also moved into strategy and product leadership in app development.

I’ve been immersed in the world of full-throttle app-making ever since the smartphone wave hit the landscape. I’m passionate about technology and volunteer with Coding Pirates to pass on the same enthusiasm to youngsters.

I have built a wide array of apps, software, and IT projects in areas of entertainment, healthcare, productivity, culture, and industry solutions. I have also started my own projects with great success. Technical leadership, mobile, web, design, and cloud technology are my specialties, and I have deep practical experience with business development, solid estimation, and marketing strategies.

I have a drive to get involved with both good ideas and passionate people, bringing both technical hands-on involvement, product development, and the ability to break any implementation down to each of its executable parts.

I can play both the leader, the architect, the planner, the builder, and the guy with a good idea. Or all at once.

So, let’s play.
