    Runion Neo

    Two typefaces, Neo and Simplex, built from straight lines between dots on a 3 × 7 grid: Latin with rune-vibes, plus the whole Unicode Runic block. Light to Bold, free under the SIL Open Font License. You are reading them now.

    type design · python · open source ↗

    A lucid dreaming tool and dream journal for Android: reality checks, dream clues and dream patterns. Over a million installs, rated 4.4 from more than 21,000 dreamers.
