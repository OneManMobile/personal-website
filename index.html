(02) products

ᚷ
  1. now

    Endavu

    Denmark’s new investment platform: more than 30,000 stocks and ETFs at some of the market’s lowest prices, automatic tax reporting, and a community where investors share what they buy. I work on it as a product engineer.

    fintech · web · android · endavu.com ↗

  2. 2013 →

    Awoken

    A lucid dreaming tool and dream journal for Android: reality checks, dream clues and dream patterns. Over a million installs, rated 4.4 by more than 21,000 dreamers.

    android · kotlin · google play ↗

  3. 2026

    Futuruna

    A programming language for law. Laws, contracts and policies become code you can run, test and audit — mixed freely with ordinary programs, and compiled through Rust. So far it holds the Danish Constitution and the Danish personal income tax act.

    language design · rust · ai · futuruna.com ↗

(03) projects

ᛈ
  1. 2026

    Sylvester

    Sylvester Chatwell, a household butler for Denmark that lives inside Grok Bot. It learns what your home eats, plans next week’s dinners around the current offers on nemlig.com, and fills the basket.

    agents · groceries · open source ↗

  2. 2024

    Antropi

    An exploration of AI built on causal entropy: the idea that intelligence seeks to maximise its future freedom of action. One of its ideas: ants with Bayesian networks, walking a grid.

    ai research · kotlin · open source ↗
